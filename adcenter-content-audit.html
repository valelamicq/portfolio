Overall verdict: V2 preserves approximately 85–90% of V1's content. The visual upgrade is significant and the core narrative holds. However, four pieces of content are genuinely absent and should be restored before this becomes the foundation for remaining case studies.

The most important items to restore are: (1) the scalability bottleneck paragraph in Challenge — it makes the business case and elevates the project; (2) "I recognized the opportunity early and made the case" — it demonstrates initiative; and (3) the current project status in Impact — it signals ongoing ownership and forward planning. These three are the difference between a project retrospective and a senior-level case study.

Once those are restored, V2 will be both more beautiful and more complete than V1.
